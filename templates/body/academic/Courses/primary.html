Schedule & Class Duration

Class Duration: 40 minutes per live interactive session.

Class Frequency: Regular weekly customized slots.

Flexible Timing: Classes are scheduled entirely according to the child's convenience and timezone.

Academy Guidelines & Rules

  • Parent Guidance: Parents of Grade 1 & 2 students are recommended to assist during the initial sessions.
  • Rescheduling Policy: Minimum 4 hours prior notice is required for canceling or rescheduling a session.
  • Technical Requirements: Stable internet connection, webcam enabled, and Zoom/Google Meet installed.
  • Homework: Short 15-minute practice worksheets assigned weekly for concept reinforcement.

Teaching Methodology

  • Visual Learning: Using engaging slides, animations, and flashcards to maintain child interest.
  • Monthly Progress Reports: Detailed assessment shared with parents on the 30th of every month.
  • Supportive Environment: Stress-free, friendly 1-on-1 atmosphere encouraging curiosity.
