Schedule & Class Duration

Class Duration: 40 minutes per live interactive session.

Class Frequency: Regular weekly customized slots.

Flexible Timing: Classes are scheduled entirely according to the student's convenience and timezone.

Academy Guidelines & Rules

  • Student Independence: Students are encouraged to manage their notes and active Q&A during sessions.
  • Rescheduling Policy: Minimum 4 hours prior notice is required for canceling or rescheduling a session.
  • Technical Requirements: Stable internet connection, webcam enabled, and Zoom/Google Meet installed.
  • Homework & Practice: Weekly practice sets assigned to build exam readiness and conceptual clarity.

Teaching Methodology

  • Concept-Focused Teaching: Breakdown of complex scientific and algebraic concepts into easy steps.
  • Monthly Progress Reports: Detailed academic evaluation shared with parents on the 30th of every month.
  • Interactive Problem Solving: Live whiteboard exercises to build student confidence.
